GYMFIT PLATFORM SLIDE 02
Product Architecture

Single Platform, Dual Personas: Member & Trainer Experience

Why a Unified Dual-Role App?

  • ✨ Zero App Store Fragmentation Eliminates the cost and confusion of deploying separate 'Staff' and 'Member' apps. One download handles both workflows.
  • 🔄 Instant One-Tap Role Switch Staff can preview member flows and members with coaching credentials can toggle into their trainer hub seamlessly.
  • ⚡ Real-Time State Synchronization Bookings, check-in scans, and schedule modifications instantly update both parties with zero lag.
  • 🎯 Motivating Onboarding Flow Dynamic athlete imagery and goal selection guide first-time users straight into their personalized fitness journey.
